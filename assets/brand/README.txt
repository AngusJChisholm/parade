PARADE LOGO SUITE

The mark: a volt (#D2FF3C) square with rounded corners (4/22 of its width), then "Parade" in Geist Bold, outlined.

Logo
- parade-logo-primary: ink text, volt square. Use on white and light backgrounds (default).
- parade-logo-reversed: white text, volt square. Use on black, dark and photo backgrounds.
- parade-logo-black / parade-logo-white: one colour, for print, embossing or where volt won't work.
Symbol
- The volt square on its own: favicon, app icon, small spaces. Never stretch it or square off the corners.
Profile pictures (1080 x 1080)
- avatar-white is the default for Instagram and LinkedIn. avatar-symbol for very small placements.
Favicon and app icon, Banners and share images: ready-sized.

Rules
- Clear space around the logo = the height of the square on every side.
- Minimum size: 80 px wide on screen.
- Don't recolour the square, add shadows or outlines, or set "Parade" in another font.
- Colours: Volt #D2FF3C, Ink #111111, White #FFFFFF.
