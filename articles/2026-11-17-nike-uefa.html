Nike's on the ball.

Nike × UEFA

FootballMatch ball supplierGlobal
Photo: Unsplash

From 2027, one ball will roll through every UEFA men's club competition, and it will carry a swoosh.

UC3, the commercial arm behind UEFA's club competitions, has named Nike the official match ball provider for the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League, along with the Super Cup, Youth League and Futsal Champions League. The partnership runs for four seasons, from 2027-28 through to 2031. No fee was disclosed by either side.

The headline detail is the bundling. It will be the first time all three major UEFA men's club competitions share a single ball provider, so a set of separate supplier slots becomes one platform. Nike Football's Vice President and General Manager Camilo Andrade put it plainly: "This is a long-term commitment for Nike." UC3 Co-Managing Director Guy-Laurent Epstein called Nike the official match ball provider for UEFA men's club competitions from 2027.

Why does a ball matter this much? It is the one piece of kit that is in shot for every minute of every match, from a cold early-round night in the Conference League to the Champions League final. Official balls are also products in their own right, sold to fans and clubs, which gives the rights holder's partner a steady run of launch moments across a season.

For the category, it is a reminder that the big sportswear brands still see real value in the rights that sit underneath the game as well as in clubs and athletes. A competition-wide ball partnership reaches every team in every round, whoever wins.

What to watch next: the first Nike ball reveal ahead of 2027-28, how Nike handles a separate design for the final, and whether the women's club competitions follow the men's into a single-supplier model.

The ball is about to become Nike's biggest four-year product launch in club football.
