Sneakers hit rugby league.

JD Sports × NZ Rugby League

Rugby leagueMajor partnerANZ
Photo: Unsplash

JD Sports just signed its first-ever national rugby league partnership, and chose New Zealand Rugby League to do it.

JD becomes Major Partner and Official Sneaker Supplier to the Kiwis, Kiwi Ferns and Wheelchair Kiwis ahead of the Rugby League World Cup, with exclusive playing-day product and content tied to the tournament.

Sneaker brands have mostly chased football, basketball and the NBA/NFL orbit. A first national rugby league partnership says the category now sees real commercial upside in the code, with the World Cup's global stage probably doing the heavy lifting.

Expect other footwear brands to take notice.

Rugby league might be about to become footwear's next battleground.
