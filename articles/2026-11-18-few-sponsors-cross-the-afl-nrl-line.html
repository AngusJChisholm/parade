Few sponsors cross the AFL-NRL line.

Brand overlap across 33 AFL and NRL clubs

DataMulti-sportANZ
Photo: Unsplash

Across 33 AFL and NRL clubs we count 1,129 sponsor brands, and only 74 of them back both codes.

Footy's two big codes chase the same Australian fans, but their sponsors rarely overlap. We took the current partner lists for 17 AFL clubs and 16 NRL clubs from the Sport Sport Sport database and counted every brand once per club. The AFL clubs list 462 different brands and the NRL clubs list 741. Put them together and you get 1,129 brands. Only 74 of them appear in both codes, which is about 7 per cent.

Those 74 punch above their weight. Between them they fill 293 of the 1,467 partner slots across the 33 clubs, or 20 per cent. So the brands that cross the line are the big, broad-reach names, while the rest of the list is sold code by code and club by club.

McDonald's is the clearest case. It sits at eight AFL clubs and eight NRL clubs, 16 in all. The Brisbane Lions announced a five-year extension to 2030, with McDonald's as Premier Partner of the men's team. Over in the NRL, the One New Zealand Warriors announced on 1 November 2026 that McDonald's had joined as official restaurant partner on a three-year term.

Elastoplast splits just as evenly, with six AFL clubs and six NRL clubs. The Canterbury-Bankstown Bulldogs named it official sports tape partner for the 2025 season, for at least two seasons, and the Warriors renewed it for two more years in December 2025. Gatorade is the biggest name on the shared list at 17 clubs, but it is an AFL story first: 15 AFL clubs list it, against two in the NRL (the Dolphins and the Warriors). The Warriors made it Official Hydration Partner from the 2025 season.

Eleven of the 74 shared brands sit at three or more clubs in each code. Between them, McDonald's, Gatorade and Elastoplast reach 27 of the 33 clubs. For a brand wanting both fan bases, the club-by-club route is slow, and few have done it.

A few limits apply. Port Adelaide, Parramatta and Tasmania are left out because their partner pages gave us ten names or fewer at the last check, and Perth Bears are out until they play. Every club included was checked between 2 and 10 November 2026. NRL clubs list more small suppliers than AFL clubs, so read the overlap as a guide, not a league table. Brands are counted as named, and the figures cover our database, not the whole market.

Few brands pay twice to reach footy fans.
